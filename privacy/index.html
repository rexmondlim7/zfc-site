Privacy

How we handle your details, in plain words.

Your application

Only ZFC and the trainer who coaches you read your answers, to arrange your coaching and plan it safely around any injury or condition.

The form runs on Tally, and answers may be stored overseas.

If you apply and don't start training, we delete your answers within 12 months.

AI and your check-ins

This part is for Rexmond's clients. I'm Rexmond, ZFC's founder. I write my clients' check-ins and reminders with help from an AI tool: Claude, made by Anthropic.

What it sees: short notes I type after a session, such as how it went or an injury we're working around. You go in as a code, never your name or number. Your form answers don't go in.

Where it runs: Anthropic's servers outside Singapore, mainly in the US. Singapore's privacy law does not bind Anthropic there; its own privacy policy does (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). I've switched off the setting that lets Anthropic train its models on my chats.

How long: I delete the notes and chats within 7 days. Anthropic may keep deleted chats a little longer under its policy.

The messages: I read every one and send it myself.

Your choice: say no, or change your mind any time, by WhatsApp. I stop straight away, and your training stays the same.

See, delete or ask

To see or delete what we hold about you, or to ask anything, message ZFC on WhatsApp.
